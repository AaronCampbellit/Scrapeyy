## Summary

Capture pages, screenshots and media; schedule captures; explicitly export selected signed-in sessions locally.

## Description

Scrapeyy captures tables, repeated cards, text, links and design references from pages you select. Preview results in a local Inbox, then export JSON, CSV, a reference ZIP or screenshots. Copy images to the clipboard, inspect exposed video files and export available captions/transcripts. Reuse recipes or opt into scheduled captures while your browser runs. SharePoint tools read metadata from your signed-in site.

Design and media tools may retrieve assets from their websites; HTTPS asset requests and SharePoint queries may use your existing website session. Wistia metadata/captions are requested anonymously. No developer analytics or remote upload service is included. HTTP-page content and screenshots remain supported, with HTTPS-only follow-up downloads and labelled fallbacks for already-loaded images/captions. Private windows are not supported.

Optional **Borrow session** exports explicitly approved sites' authentication cookies (including HttpOnly cookies) and browser storage to a local file for manual use in another VM/project. This file can grant account access. Encryption is the default; plaintext requires a deliberate choice. Cookie/site permissions and approval are required, and session secrets are not stored in the Inbox.

No payment is required. Captures and exports may contain sensitive or copyrighted website content; inspect them before sharing. Cross-origin frames, protected assets, virtualized content and streaming-only media can limit capture. Direct media downloads are limited to 512 MiB/two minutes and may require the site's download controls when access or redirects prevent retrieval.
